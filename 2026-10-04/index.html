1. Summary

Integrated build of the open boot stack (TF-A, OP-TEE, U-Boot, Linux at EL2 with KVM, Buildroot) on four Qualcomm boards, with every firmware component freshly built and fingerprinted, run through the complete open-boot-stack test plan in LAVA: the full plan twice per target (and per boot flow on the VENTUNO Q), one job per maxcpus value, memtest, the negative controls, and the Arm SystemReady DT band ACS. This run integrates the fastrpc 1.0.8 RPC poll mode (F19), Adreno firmware and the GPU userspace for the RB3 Gen 2 and IQ-9075 EVK, Mesa 25.2.8 with the TensorFlow Lite pitch fix, and the UNO Q socinfo change. The VENTUNO Q and RB3 Gen 2 full plans, maxcpus jobs and KVM negative controls were rerun with the PMU PPI-partition device tree fix added to their kernels (same signed firmware); the first-pass jobs are kept as superseded. new VENTUNO Q NPU latency in the inference test is async queue-inflated: the NPU itself takes 0.40 ms per inference (INF-1)

On the v75 NSP qnn-net-run runs asynchronously through dspqueue and queues inputs ahead, so QNN's per-inference time includes the wait behind the earlier queued inferences. That is why the measured VENTUNO Q npu0 mean ranges from 1.81 to 8.09 ms with p90 up to 4.1 times p50 across this run's full plans. A FastRPC timing shim shows 560 async inferences finish in 0.35 to 0.37 s of wall-clock time while QNN's per-inference times sum to 3.7 to 4.1 s (470186, 470187). Run synchronously (qnn-net-run --synchronous), the same model takes 0.398 to 0.406 ms per inference on the VENTUNO Q (470190, 470191: mean 0.401 ms, p50 0.400, p90 0.406), faster than the IQ-9075 EVK (1.74 ms) and the RB3 Gen 2 (0.70 ms), which do not run async (their sync and async numbers agree: 470203, 470347). The test's VENTUNO Q NPU numbers are therefore a measure of queue depth, not of the NPU, the kernel or the firmware. The fix (--synchronous in the inference test) is drafted, not yet applied.

14. Reproduce

Everything below uses the repositories listed in section 7. Commits marked local, not yet published must be published first; until then this run cannot be rebuilt from public sources alone. In particular the PMU PPI-partition patches are carried in qcom-buildroot (local branch), the OP-TEE patches of the RB3 Gen 2 and IQ-9075 EVK and two Linux patches of the IQ-9075 EVK are applied by the build kit from patch files, and the VENTUNO Q OP-TEE (monaco-pas-v3) and U-Boot (ventuno-q-v3-fdtfile) branches are local.

  1. Workspace: repo init -u https://github.com/EmbeddedAndroid/qcom-manifest -b <branch> -m <board>.xml with the build project replaced by qcom-buildroot integration-20261004. The published ventuno-q-v3 branch has monaco.xml; the agatti.xml, kodiak.xml and lemans.xml pins of this run are on the local all-boards branch (local, not yet published). Toolchains: make toolchains (Arm GNU Toolchain 14.3.Rel1), in an Ubuntu 24.04 build container.
  2. VENTUNO Q: override OP-TEE with monaco-pas-v3 and U-Boot with ventuno-q-v3-fdtfile, then make all TZ_IMAGE=u-boot-spl BUILD_ID=<SPL ID> and make all BUILD_ID=<BL2 ID>, plus the efi variants with LINUX_CMDLINE="... maxcpus=N", memtest=4 and maxcpus=2 kvm-arm.mode=none. QTI-sign the TZ image with make tz-qti-sign.
  3. RB3 Gen 2: make all BUILD_ID=<ID> with the OP-TEE PAS patches applied, then make tz-qti-sign. IQ-9075 EVK: make bootimage efi with TEE_IMPL_VERSION, LOCALVERSION and TF_A_FLAGS=... BUILD_STRING set to the build ID.
  4. UNO Q: TF-A imola (PLAT=uno_q SPD=opteed BL2 and FIP with OP-TEE PLATFORM=qcom-agatti and U-Boot qcom_defconfig tfa-optee.config arduino-uno-q.config), the arduino/linux-qcom kernel with the PAS series, and an ESP made from the VENTUNO Q userspace with the UNO Q kernel modules. QTI-sign BL2.
  5. Package each board's qcomflash (stock boot binaries with tz, uefi and efi replaced) and generate the jobs from the plan: plans/open-boot-stack in test-definitions, one full job, one job per maxcpus value, memtest=4 and the negative control; the SystemReady job flashes the ACS ESP and root filesystem with the board's DTB as /dtb/<fdtfile> (VENTUNO Q: qcom/monaco-arduino-monza.dtb).
  6. Submit to LAVA and compare by differencing two runs' measurements (get_job_results with limit 2000).

The job generators are not published yet; the job definitions of this run are public in LAVA (every job link in section 7) and can be resubmitted after replacing the artifact URLs.
